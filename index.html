About

Turning raw data into decisions

I'm a final-year B.Tech Computer Science Engineering student at Synergy Institute, affiliated with Biju Patnaik University of Technology (BPUT), Rourkela. My core interest lies in data analytics and data science — figuring out what a dataset is actually trying to say, and turning that into something a decision-maker can act on.

Alongside that, I work with AI and machine learning to build practical, intelligent tools — from automated data-profiling systems to NLP-based applications. I'm not claiming to be an expert; I'm a student who learns by building, breaking, and rebuilding real projects, and I'm currently preparing for internships, placements, and hackathons like Smart India Hackathon.

What I care about most: asking the right question of a dataset, cleaning it honestly, visualizing it clearly, and explaining what it means in plain language.
